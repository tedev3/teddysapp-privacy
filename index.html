Teddy's App — Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 · Teddy's App is built and operated by Tim Devereux · teddys.app.beta@gmail.com

This policy explains what Teddy's App is, what information it stores, who can see that information, how it is protected, and the choices you have.

What Teddy's App is

Teddy's App is a private memory-keeping app for family and close friends. I built it to capture Memories and tell the stories of my son Teddy's childhood, but it can hold any Memory that matters to you: a birthday, a trip, a special night out, the first day of school.

The app is built around Moments and Memories. Moments are individual photos and videos from your phone, or notes and voice recordings you create in the app. Memories are collections of Moments arranged to tell the story of something you want to remember. You decide which Memories to share, and with whom.

Teddy's App is a closed circle, not social media. There is no public profile and no way for anyone to search for or discover you. The only people who can see a Memory of yours are people you have invited and then chosen to share that Memory with.

What the app stores, and where

When you add a photo or video to Teddy's App, the app makes its own copy of it. The original is not moved, changed or deleted; it stays where it was, in your camera roll or in your iCloud. The app's copy is made for the app: a photo is resized, and a video is kept at full quality on your phone and sent to the server at a smaller size. Neither copy carries the hidden information stored inside the original file, such as where the photo or video was taken or which device took it; the app keeps only the date the photo or video was taken.

The photos and videos you upload, and everything else you put into a Memory, are stored on a server I run. A server is what lets a Memory you share appear on someone else's phone. The server stores:

  • the photos, videos, voice recordings and notes you add to Memories;
  • the titles, captions, dates, place names and tags you give them;
  • the people you add to the app: their name, their relationship to you, their birthday if you add one, the groups you put them in, and the photos and Memories you mark them as being in;
  • which Memories you have shared with which people and groups;
  • the reactions and comments you leave on Memories;
  • the name and email address that Apple provides when you sign in with Apple;
  • a notification token for your phone, so the app can send you notifications;
  • the date and version of the privacy note you agreed to in the app.

What the app does not collect

The app does not collect anything on its own. It does not read your phone's location, your contacts or your address book, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. Every piece of information on the server is something you added or wrote yourself. One detail worth knowing: when you add a place to a Memory and start typing, the app suggests matching places using Apple Maps, so the words you type are sent to Apple to produce the suggestions. What is saved with the Memory is just the text you chose.

Who can see what you add

Nothing you add is visible to anyone until you share that Memory. Sharing is done one Memory at a time, to the specific people or groups of people (the app calls them Circles) you pick, and you can stop sharing at any time. When you stop sharing a Memory, it disappears from the other person's app the next time their app refreshes, which it does each time it is opened. People you share with can see the Memory, react to it and comment on it, and their reactions and comments are visible to everyone that Memory is shared with. They can keep the Memory in their own Memories list while you share it; it remains yours, and it leaves their list if you stop sharing. If you make someone a contributor to a Memory, they can add their own Moments to it; what they add stays theirs, and either of you can remove it. People you are connected with can also see your name and your profile picture, as you can see theirs. They cannot see your other Memories, your circle, or anything else in your account.

I do not sell your information, show you advertising, or use your photos or words to train AI or to build any other product. The rules about who can see what are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app, and an automated test suite checks them.

Encryption, and what I can see

Your information is encrypted while it travels to the server and while it is stored there. Right now, while the app is still in development, it is not end-to-end encrypted. That means that as the person who runs the server, I can technically see anything you upload, as the developer of almost any app can. I have no intention of viewing anything you have uploaded, except to fix a problem you have asked me to fix, or if you choose to share a Memory with me. To keep the service running I do look at technical information about it, such as whether the server is healthy, how much storage is in use, and when each account last connected, but not at anyone's photos or words.

When the app is released in the App Store, all photos, videos, notes and voice recordings will be encrypted end to end, so that even I, or anyone who works on this project in the future, cannot see them. Until then, please only add and share what you would be happy sending in a family group text.

Information about other people, including children

The app lets you add other people to your own records: a name, how they are related to you, a birthday if you choose to add one, and which of your photos they are in. Everything you enter about a person stays in your account. It is not shown to that person, it is not shown to the people you share with, and it is not combined with what anyone else has entered about them.

If a person has their own Teddy's App account and you connect with them, their name and picture come from their own account; your relationship label and anything else you entered stay yours. Children do not have accounts. A child exists in the app only as a person that a parent or another adult has added to their own records.

Because you are the one adding this information, you are responsible for having the right to add it. For an adult, that means they are okay with it. For a child, that means you are their parent or guardian, or have a parent's permission. Teddy's App is for adults aged 18 and over.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app tells you about two things only: when someone shares a Memory with you, and when someone comments on a Memory of yours or one you have already commented on. Notifications are delivered through Apple's notification service. A notification contains the person's name and the title of the Memory, and nothing else: never the photo, never the words of the comment. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Your choices and controls

  • Sharing. You decide which Memories to share and with whom, and you can stop sharing any Memory at any time. You can also remove a person from your circle.
  • Export. From inside the app you can export all of your Memories, or any single one, as ordinary folders of photos, videos and text.
  • Delete your account. "Delete account" in Profile erases your account and everything stored for it on the server, including every photo, video and recording.
  • Notifications. You can turn them off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Questions and requests. For anything else, including "please delete my information", email teddys.app.beta@gmail.com.

Where your data is stored, and who processes it

The server is hosted with Supabase, on computers located in Oregon, in the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, your data is still stored in the United States. The server is backed up every day; backups are kept for about a week and then replaced.

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, delivers notifications, and provides the place-name suggestions described above. During the test period Apple also provides TestFlight, and shares crash reports and basic statistics about the test with me. Apple handles those services under its own terms. No other company receives your data.

During the test

Teddy's App is currently in a closed test for invited people aged 18 and over. During the test, please keep your camera roll as your main copy of everything: the app holds copies and should not be your only copy of anything. If the test ends, I will tell you, and the data on the server will be deleted unless you choose to continue with the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, this page will be updated, the app will ask you to read the new version before you continue, and I will tell you directly.
