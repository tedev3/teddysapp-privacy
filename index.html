Teddy's App — Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 August 2026 · Contact: tedevereux@gmail.com

Teddy's App is a private memory-keeping app for family and close friends, built and operated by

Tim Devereux. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and the controls you

have. It is written in plain English on purpose. It says the same things as the beta consent

you agreed to when you joined the test.

What the app stores

Your photos and videos stay in your camera roll — the app works from imported copies, it never

moves or deletes your originals.

When you put something into a memory, it syncs to our private server (hosted with Supabase, on

servers in Oregon, United States) so it can reach the people you choose to share with:

  • photos, videos, voice recordings, and notes you add;
  • titles, captions, dates, and place names you write;
  • the people you add to your circle — names, relationships, and birthdates if you enter them (including children's, if you choose to);
  • your Apple sign-in email address and display name;
  • reactions and comments you leave;
  • a push-notification token for your device, so the app can tell you when someone shares with you.

Nothing is collected automatically. The app never reads your location (no GPS), never reads

your contacts or address book, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of

any kind. A place name on a memory is text you typed, nothing more.

Who can see what

Nothing you add is visible to anyone until you deliberately share that memory. Sharing is

per-memory, to the specific people or groups you pick. There is no public content, no strangers,

no algorithm, and no ads.

**We never sell your information, never show you advertising, and never use your content to

train AI or any other product.** Access rules are enforced in the database itself and are

covered by an automated test suite.

The honest limit

Your data is encrypted on the way to the server and while it is stored there. But the app is

not end-to-end encrypted — in plain terms, the developer could technically access what is

stored (this is true of most cloud apps), and would only ever do so to debug a problem, with

your knowledge.

The people you add

You may add other people's names, photos, and details to your memories. Please only do that

with their OK — and for anything about a child, only if you are their parent or guardian, or

have a parent's OK. Nothing about the people you add is collected automatically; every detail

is your explicit, optional entry.

Your controls

  • Share on your terms. Sharing is always per-memory and can be undone.
  • Export anytime. From inside the app you can export your whole journal, or any single memory, as ordinary folders of photos and text.
  • Delete account (in Profile) erases everything you stored on the server, including all media files.
  • Leave anytime by deleting the app. For questions, problems, or "please delete my stuff," email tedevereux@gmail.com.

Where your data lives, and who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (our hosting processor) on servers in the United States (Oregon).

If you use the app from outside the US, your data is still stored in the US. Apple processes

Sign in with Apple, and — during the beta — TestFlight, under Apple's own terms (Apple shares

crash logs and basic beta statistics with the developer). No other third party receives your data.

The beta

The current release is a closed test for invited testers aged 18 or over. The server is backed

up daily, but keep your originals in your camera roll — the app holds copies and is not your

only copy of anything. If the beta ends, you will be told, and server data will be deleted

unless you continue.

Changes

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, this page will be updated and testers will be

told directly.
